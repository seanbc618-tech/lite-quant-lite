## What the accounting verifies

At each observation the ledger marks existing units, sizes trades from current
equity, applies sells before buys, deducts per-side costs, and checks that equity
after trading equals equity before trading minus cost. Final liquidation is
charged too. Eight observations are ordinal synthetic steps, not trading dates.
The rule is fixed, so training/validation/test partitions are not applicable.
Input and protocol SHA-256 hashes are embedded in the JSON.

## Limitations / 限制

- Immediate full fills at the supplied price are simulated assumptions; there
  is no exchange connection, fill reconciliation or settlement model.
- Fees reduce equity after target sizing; target weights therefore differ
  slightly from post-cost holdings. The 20% cash reserve permits the specified
  examples to pay fees, but does not prove arbitrary portfolios are affordable.
- The inherited helper rounds order notionals to cents and suppresses trades
  at or below the threshold. Final liquidation uses full fractional value;
  this is an accounting simplification, not a supported broker order format.
- The inherited helper ignores non-positive weights/current holdings, does not
  support an all-cash target, and lacks complete handling of NaN, infinity and
  symbol collisions after normalization. It is reused unchanged and receives
  controlled inputs only. Five inherited tests are not exhaustive assurance.
- No slippage, spreads, taxes, liquidity, lot sizes, corporate actions, interest,
  or borrow constraints are modeled. No private strategy is evaluated.
- Exact output was verified on the recorded Python environment. Other Python
  versions/platforms are not claimed verified. Checksums detect changes, not
  independent provenance or scientific validity.
- Historical negative research is reviewed separately in
  [HISTORICAL_REVIEW.md](HISTORICAL_REVIEW.md); it is not evidence generated by
  this synthetic simulation. Historical raw data and private parameters are
  intentionally not redistributed, so full independent historical replication
  is unavailable in this public candidate.

# Project introduction

**Point-in-Time Data & Portfolio Lab**

Offline financial-data normalization, filing-time and label-boundary audits,
and portfolio accounting with reproducible synthetic examples.

An offline demonstration of financial-data normalization, temporal availability,
label-boundary auditing and portfolio accounting, reused from a US-equity
research codebase. Synthetic examples make the engineering evidence reproducible
while retaining gaps, failures and methodological limits. This is not a claim of
predictive validity or production readiness.

Codex assisted this preparation. This description concerns the artifact, not
unverified personal duties, independent authorship, original invention, adoption
or investment performance.
